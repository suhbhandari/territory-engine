## Built, not proven yet

| Item | What exists | What proving it needs |
|---|---|---|
| Licence check for every account | The `state_license` signal (`src/signals/open_data_match.py`) matches each account to the state's list by phone, exact name, or a near-identical name in the same zip; the rubric uses the result. | Review the 29 tier A and B groups with `not_found`, and check a larger sample of matches. So far 8 of 8 matches and 6 of 6 `not_found` accounts were right (14 accounts). A name that differs from the state's list stays `not_found`. |
| Licence matching by brand name | An exact name match does not check the zip, so a brand name can pull in one franchisee's licence for a different location. Three locations of one national franchise brand all matched a single licensee; three locations of another matched three unrelated operators. Measured 2026-10-13: 31 of 532 matched accounts share a licence record this way, affecting 6 of the 244 tier A and B groups. | Require the zip or the phone to agree when a name matches more than one account, or when the matched record's operator name differs from the account name. Then re-score, because the result feeds the agency-type criterion. |
| A register's category used as a company name | A brief wrote the register's filing category as the subject of a sentence, as if it were the business's name. Measured 2026-10-13: 1 of the 244 published briefs. A prompt rule now forbids it, but nothing checks it, so it can come back silently. It did: the 2026-10-22 hand grading found it in one sequence (an email and a call opener). | Make it a check: no value from a column listed in config may be used as a proper-noun subject. The checker would need the account's row, which check_statements does not currently receive. |
| Name matching misses spelled-out legal forms | `name_key` strips llc, inc and corp but not "Limited Liability Company" written out, so a register entry and an account that are plainly the same business fail to match. Found by `make recall` on 2026-10-13, which also showed the engine missing about 24% of the licensed providers in its own zip codes. | Add the spelled-out forms to the legal-suffix list, then re-run `make recall` and see how much of the gap was matching rather than discovery. |
| Weekly refresh | `make refresh`. Measured: run on 2026-10-22 against the 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-13 collection, with no discover between. 15 of 1,501 accounts changed tier; 12 briefs and 12 sequences were rewritten because their facts changed, and 3 of each were written for groups new to tiers A and B, for $0.88. Numbers in the report card's "Week over week" section. | More pairs, to see whether the rate of change is steady. Some of the change may be noise rather than change at the account (a website that answered one week and not the next); that has not been separated. |
| Trigger list | `make triggers` and `triggers.yaml`. Measured on the same pair: 55 events on 32 accounts (website responds 22, tier 15, hiring evidence 9, hiring system 5, open roles 4); 14 of those accounts are in tiers A or B. | A rep says whether the changes it lists are worth acting on. |
| Monday playbook | `docs/monday-playbook.md` | A rep follows it for a week; fix what breaks. |
| Second vertical | `config/verticals/home_services/` ran on a 137-account Phoenix sample on 2026-10-22 with no core code changes, after #15 and #16 were done (19 core files). `docs/second-vertical.md` lists every file and why. | The run shows the vertical's main signal is nearly empty: 6 of 137 contractors publish a readable job feed. Needs a signal that does not depend on careers pages before the ranking means much; then a person checks the top of the list. |
| Evals with people | 15 briefs and drafts graded by hand on 2026-10-22 (`outputs/private/hand_grades.md`), compared with both model rounds by `make agreement`; the agreement table is in the report card. Person and model agree on usefulness and disagree on accuracy (person 2.7 and 2.0 against model 4.5 and 4.0 for briefs and drafts), mostly because the person checked the live source pages and the model checked only the cited facts. | A second person grading the same 15, to see whether the gap is the grader or the scale. A written definition of accuracy that says which of those two checks it means. An LLM judge prompted with that definition, measured against the hand grades. |
| Prompt versions | `outputs/raw/first_pass_log.json` (private, kept by hand) records first-attempt check results with failure types for the 2026-10-12 and 2026-10-13 runs. | The brief and sequence indexes still keep only the final pass. Log a prompt version and first-pass failures per item in the indexes. |
